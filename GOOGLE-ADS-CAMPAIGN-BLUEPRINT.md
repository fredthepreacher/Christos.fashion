# Christos.Fashion — Initial Google Ads Campaign Blueprint

## Goal
Generate first-party ecommerce sales while learning which Christian-apparel search intents convert profitably.

## Primary Conversion
**Purchase** — value-based, passed from successful Stripe payment.

Secondary observations: add_to_cart, begin_checkout, view_item.

## Bidding / Launch Discipline

- Do not optimize to add-to-cart as the primary business result once purchase tracking is working.
- Avoid aggressive automation before enough purchase data exists.
- Keep phrase/exact intent tight at launch; expand only from demonstrated search terms.
- Compare search-term profitability by product family rather than assuming a marketplace trend will automatically convert for Christos.Fashion.

## Merchant / Shopping Phase

After Merchant Center approval and verified purchase conversion tracking:
- enable free listings;
- launch Shopping or Performance Max with a controlled test;
- use Merchant feed custom labels to separate Therapy, Faith Over Fear, Jesus Saves, and evergreen inventory;
- exclude products with weak imagery, incomplete variants, or policy/feed diagnostics until corrected.
