Every Design Has a Reason

The Message
Behind the Design.

We don't print words because they look good on cotton. Each one was chosen for a specific moment a believer walks through. Here's what each design says — and who it's for.

Built by Faith

"Nothing in my life was built by me alone."

Why it matters
Achievement culture tells you to take the credit. This is a quiet correction — a reminder, worn on your chest, of who actually did the building.
Who it's for
The believer in a season of growth who wants to stay grounded while it happens.
Wear it with Work Hard, God First Jesus Saves Hat

Faith Over Fear

"Fear showed up first. Faith gets the final word."

Why it matters
Courage isn't the absence of fear — it's the decision made right after it. Three words that settle the argument before you have it.
Who it's for
Anyone standing at the edge of a hard decision: a diagnosis, a move, a conversation they've been avoiding.
Wear it with I Thought It Was Over God Taught Me

I Thought It Was Over

"…but God wasn't finished."

Why it matters
The most powerful testimony in the room is usually the one nobody knows about. This starts that conversation without you having to.
Who it's for
The believer on the other side of something that nearly took them out — and anyone still in it who needs to see that other side exists.
Wear it with Faith Over Fear Send a prayer request

Jesus Saves

The two boldest words in history, and no commentary needed.

Why it matters
No hedging, no clever wordplay. Worn on a cap it sits at eye level — the first thing someone reads when they look up at you.
Who it's for
The believer who's done being subtle about it. Also the easiest first piece if you're new to wearing your faith.
Wear it with Any tee in the collection

Pray. Work. Repeat.

"Discipline is worship you can schedule."

Why it matters
Faith isn't only for the mountaintop. It's the rhythm of an ordinary Tuesday — pray, then go do the work, then do it again tomorrow.
Who it's for
Early risers, gym regulars, students, and anyone building something slowly and faithfully.
Wear it with Work Hard, God First Built by Faith

Work Hard, God First

"Ambition is fine. Order matters."

Why it matters
It doesn't ask you to want less. It asks you to keep the sequence right — which is the harder discipline of the two.
Who it's for
Founders, hustlers, and anyone whose calendar has started quietly outranking their prayer life.
Wear it with Pray. Work. Repeat. Jesus Saves Hat

God Taught Me

"The lesson cost something. I'd still take it."

Why it matters
Some things you only learn in the hard year. This honours that without pretending it was easy.
Who it's for
The believer who came out of a difficult season changed rather than bitter.
Wear it with I Thought It Was Over Faith Over Fear

Christ Over Culture

"Every generation is asked to choose. This is the answer."

Why it matters
Not a shot at anyone — a statement of allegiance. When the two point different directions, you already know which way you're going.
Who it's for
The believer holding a conviction that isn't currently popular, and holding it with grace.
Wear it with Faith Over Fear Jesus Saves Hat

The Fit

Classic unisex cut with a straight body — true to size for a relaxed fit, size down for something closer. Sizes XS–5XL across the tee range.

The Fabric

Soft, durable cotton chosen so the shirt keeps its shape and the print keeps its edges. Made to be worn weekly, not saved for special occasions.

The Care

Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colours. Tumble dry low. Skip the iron on the print. That's the whole routine.

The Delivery

Printed on demand when you order, so nothing sits in a warehouse. US orders over $50 ship free; orders under $50 use a $5.99 standard shipping rate. Made-to-order items are not returned for sizing or buyer's remorse, but defective, damaged, or misprinted items are covered by our 30-day replacement support.
