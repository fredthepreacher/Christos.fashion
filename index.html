The Brand

What Is Christos.Fashion?

Christos.Fashion — also known as Christ Clothing — is an online Christian apparel brand for believers who want to wear their faith, not just talk about it. We design bold, gospel-centered clothing that starts conversations and points people to Jesus. Christos (Χριστός) is the Greek word for Christ, and every design carries that name with purpose.
