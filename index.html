Why Christos

Christos.Fashion is built for normal life — work, school, the gym, church, coffee runs, and the conversations in between. Every design starts with a faith-centered truth worth carrying, then becomes apparel made to feel intentional rather than like event merch.

Common Questions

Answers, Straight Up.

Christos.Fashion is a Christian apparel brand, also known as Christ Clothing, that makes faith-based shirts and hats for believers who want to wear their faith in everyday life. Every design carries a Gospel-centred message intended to start conversations rather than simply decorate a garment.
Christos.Fashion sells Christian graphic t-shirts and statement hats, including the Jesus Saves trucker hat, Faith Over Fear, Built by Faith, Work Hard God First, Pray Work Repeat, and the Therapy Collection. Shirts are available in sizes XS through 5XL.
The Therapy Collection is a group of five Christian statement designs: Scripture Is My Therapy, Jesus Is My Therapy, God Is My Therapy, Prayer Is My Therapy, and Christ Is My Therapy. Each one points to faith as a source of hope and encouragement. They are statements of belief, not medical or mental-health advice.
Christos, written Χριστός in Greek, is the New Testament word for Christ, meaning the Anointed One. The brand carries that name because every design is built around the person of Jesus Christ.
