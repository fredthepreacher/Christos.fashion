Store Policy

Shipping
Made Clear.

What to expect between placing your order and wearing your message.

Where We Currently Ship

Our direct checkout is currently configured for delivery within the United States. We are focusing the initial Christos.Fashion launch on US customers so shipping rates, checkout, and Google Shopping information remain consistent.

Shipping Cost

Standard US shipping is $5.99 on orders under $50. Orders with a merchandise subtotal of $50 or more receive free standard US shipping. The final shipping charge is displayed before payment.

Production Time

Christos.Fashion products are made to order through our print-on-demand fulfillment partner, Printify. Production typically takes 2–7 business days before the order ships.

Transit Time

After production, standard US transit is typically another 3–5 business days. Most orders therefore arrive in approximately 5–12 business days from the order date. These are estimates rather than guaranteed delivery dates.

Tracking

When fulfillment provides tracking, a shipping notification is sent to the email address associated with the order. Carrier scans and delivery timing are controlled by the shipping carrier after the package leaves the fulfillment facility.

Address Accuracy

Please review your name, street address, apartment or unit number, city, state, and ZIP code carefully before payment. If you notice an error after ordering, contact hello@christos.fashion as quickly as possible. Once production or shipment begins, changes may no longer be possible.

Delays, Lost Packages & Returned Packages

If tracking shows an unusual delay, a package is returned to sender, or your order appears lost, contact us with your order ID. We will review the available fulfillment and carrier information and help determine the appropriate next step. Carrier delays outside our control may extend the estimated delivery window.

Questions

Email hello@christos.fashion or use our contact form.
